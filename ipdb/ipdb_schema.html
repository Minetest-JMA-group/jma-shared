What triggers a merge?
Alice logs in from Charlie's IP — entries 1 & 2 were separate. ipdb detects the shared IP and merges them into one. Before merging, all data from both entries is snapshotted into the three log tables.

Snapshots, not live references.
Log rows copy data as it was before the merge. Pruning a MergeEvent cascade-deletes all its log rows. You can always reconstruct "what did entry #2 look like before being absorbed?"
